# Fellow Ode Gen 2 grind reference

Scale **1 (finest) → 11 (coarsest)**. The Gen 2 SSP-style burrs grind ~3–4 clicks finer than Gen 1 at equivalent extraction — **the Gen 1 hopper-lid guide does not apply** (Fellow's own warning). Sweet spot for filter is roughly **4–6**.

## Default recommendations

| Mode | Light | Medium | Dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single-serve (≤500 ml) | 5 | 5 | 6 |
| Batch (>500 ml) | 5 | 6 | 6 |

## Descriptor mapping (for the brew plan)

| Grind | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | fine |
| 4 | medium-fine |
| 5 | medium |
| 6 | medium-coarse |
| 7–8 | coarse |
| 9–11 | very coarse |

## Adjustment heuristics

If the user reports a taste issue (**v2 territory**, but useful context):

| Symptom | Grind nudge |
|---|---|
| Sour / underextracted | -1 (finer) |
| Bitter / overextracted | +1 (coarser) |
| Silty / muddy cup | +1 |
| Weak / watery | -1, or ratio tighter |

## Why these numbers

Gen 2 range is 275–1160 microns vs. Gen 1's coarser floor — at the same dial number you get a finer particle. Community consensus (Coffeetime forum, Brew Commons, Fellow support articles): V60 / pourover 4.0–5.2, Aiden default profile users around 5–8 at 1:18. Brew Commons (tuned for Aiden + light roasts) recommends 3.1–4.0 for light, 4.2–5.2 for dark — but that skews fine for most palates. The table above splits the difference toward the conservative end.

Batch mode tolerates one click coarser than single-serve for medium/dark because the pulse-based full-basket extraction is more even. Light stays at 5 in both modes — light Ethiopians + anaerobic naturals reward the extra extraction.

Fresh beans (<14 days post-roast) may need +1 due to CO₂ release slowing flow.

## Migrating from Gen 1 notes

Old tasting notes taken on Gen 1 are **not directly comparable** — e.g., a bean dialed in at Gen 1 `8` is roughly Gen 2 `4–5`. Expect to re-dial everything; don't trust old grind settings.
