# Fellow Ode Gen 1 grind reference

Scale **1 (finest) → 11 (coarsest)**. The Ode Gen 1 is filter-focused; the sweet spot for drip is 4–7.

## Default recommendations

| Mode | Light | Medium | Dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single-serve (≤500 ml) | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| Batch (>500 ml) | 5 | 5 | 6 |

## Descriptor mapping (for the brew plan)

| Grind | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | fine |
| 4 | medium-fine |
| 5 | medium |
| 6 | medium-coarse |
| 7–8 | coarse |
| 9–11 | very coarse |

## Adjustment heuristics

If the user reports a taste issue (**v2 territory**, but useful context):

| Symptom | Grind nudge |
|---|---|
| Sour / underextracted | -1 (finer) |
| Bitter / overextracted | +1 (coarser) |
| Silty / muddy cup | +1 |
| Weak / watery | -1, or ratio tighter |

## Why these numbers

Fellow recommends 4–6 for pour-over / SS on the Ode. Batch brewers tolerate slightly coarser because the extraction is more even (full immersion-ish with pulses). Dark roasts extract faster → coarser to avoid bitterness. Light roasts extract slowly → finer to get the acidity out.

These are starting points. Fresh beans (<14 days post-roast) may need +1 due to CO₂ release slowing flow.
